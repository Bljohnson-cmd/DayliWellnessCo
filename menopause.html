The Gut-Hormone Axis

Your gut is running
your hormones.
Here's how.

Inside your gut lives a collection of bacteria called the estrobolome — they're responsible for processing and regulating estrogen in your body.

When estrogen declines during perimenopause, gut microbial diversity decreases too — further impairing estrogen metabolism. Women with constipation or gut imbalance may have excess estrogen reabsorbed rather than eliminated.

Prebiotic fiber feeds the beneficial bacteria in your estrobolome, supporting healthier estrogen processing and helping break the cycle of hormonal disruption.

3x

Women on high-fiber diets excreted three times more estrogen and had 15-20% lower serum estrogen levels compared to women on standard diets.

Published in peer-reviewed research on dietary fiber and estrogen metabolism.

The cycle: Hormonal changes damage the gut. A damaged gut worsens hormonal imbalance. Fiber supports the gut bacteria that help process hormones — interrupting the cycle from the inside.
