Why It Matters

One nutrient.
Whole-body impact.

Fiber isn't glamorous. But the science is clear — it quietly supports nearly every system in your body.

Gut-Skin Axis

A healthier gut microbiome is linked to clearer, more radiant skin. Prebiotic fiber feeds the bacteria that support your glow from within.

Hormonal Balance

Fiber helps your body process and clear excess hormones through the estrobolome — the gut bacteria that regulate estrogen metabolism.

Steady Energy

Soluble fiber slows glucose absorption, reducing the post-meal spikes and energy crashes that derail your afternoon.

Satiety

Fiber slows gastric emptying, keeping you comfortably full longer — without extra calories or constant snacking.

Gut Health

Prebiotic fiber feeds beneficial gut bacteria, supporting a diverse microbiome and reducing everyday bloating.

Beauty From Within

Fiber supports detoxification and reduces inflammation — two drivers of the clear, radiant skin you're after.

Fiber, Done Right

The right fiber
for the job.

Not all fiber is created equal — and different jobs call for different fibers. So we use two. One disappears into your water. The other turns fruit into jam. Both feed your gut; neither leaves you bloated.

Two fibers, two jobs

In Electrolytes + Fiber, we use a soluble fiber that dissolves completely clear — no grit, no gel, no taste. It's gentle and low-FODMAP, so it feeds your gut without the gas and bloating that make most people quit.

In JamPact, we do the opposite on purpose. Whole-food fibers like chia, psyllium, and golden flax are chosen because they gel — that's what sets real fruit into a spreadable, high-fiber jam. The texture isn't a side effect; it's the point.

  • No inulin or chicory root — the high-FODMAP fibers behind most bloating complaints
  • Soluble and insoluble fiber across the lineup, like a real-food diet
  • Prebiotic by design — fiber that feeds beneficial gut bacteria
  • Whole-food sources, not lab-isolated fillers
