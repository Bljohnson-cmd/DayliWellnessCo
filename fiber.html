Not all fiber is created equal. Most fiber supplements use ingredients that cause bloating, gas, or gritty textures. We use PHGG — a clinically studied prebiotic fiber that dissolves completely clear with zero taste.

What is PHGG?

Partially hydrolyzed guar gum (PHGG) is a soluble prebiotic fiber sourced from guar beans. Unlike psyllium or inulin, it dissolves completely in water — no grit, no gel, no bloating.

It's low-FODMAP certified, meaning it's gentle enough for sensitive stomachs. And because it doesn't ferment aggressively in the gut, you get the benefits of fiber without the gas and discomfort that make most people quit.

  • Dissolves completely clear — invisible in any drink
  • Zero taste, zero grit, zero texture change
  • Low-FODMAP certified — gentle on sensitive systems
  • Clinically studied prebiotic — feeds beneficial gut bacteria
